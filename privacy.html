Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TikTok AI Publisher Agent (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Service to publish video content to your TikTok account.

2. Information We Collect

When you authorize the Service via TikTok's official OAuth flow, we receive: your basic TikTok profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) and access tokens required to publish content on your behalf. We also process the video files, captions, and metadata you choose to upload through the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively to provide the Service: authenticating your TikTok account, displaying your profile so you can confirm the destination account before posting, and publishing the content you have reviewed and approved. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored securely and used only to perform actions you have explicitly requested. Video content is processed transiently for upload and is not retained longer than necessary to complete the publishing operation. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke the Service's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok app settings (Settings > Security > Apps and services). Upon revocation, the associated tokens become invalid. You may also request deletion of any data we hold by contacting us at the address below.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service interacts with TikTok's official APIs. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at namhk@vng.com.vn.
